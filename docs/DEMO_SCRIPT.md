# Demo video script (about 2 minutes)

Record the screen at 1080p, your own voice, no music needed. Read every number off the screen
at the time of recording; this script deliberately contains none.

Rule for the whole video: show one answer that is verified, and one that is refused. The refusal
is the product.

## 1. The problem (0:00 to 0:15)
Say: "AI analysts give you a confident number. You can't tell if it came from your data or from
the model's imagination. This one checks every number against the database, or it withholds
the answer."

## 2. A verified answer (0:15 to 0:55)
- Open the recorded run for a plain question on the sales data.
- Point at a figure in the answer. Click it: the margin note shows the exact query and the value
  it matched.
- Say what the tick means, in one sentence: the figure matches its source. Then say what it does
  not mean: it does not prove the metric is the right one to ask.

## 3. Data plus a document (0:55 to 1:20)
- Open a recorded run that combines a figure with a policy passage.
- Click the quoted passage: it shows the stored text it was matched against.

## 4. The refusal (1:20 to 1:45)
- Open a recorded run where the answer was withheld, or ask the check to fail on purpose.
- Say: "It would rather tell you it can't verify than guess."

## 5. Honest limits (1:45 to 2:00)
Say: "It verifies a number matches its source. It does not check the question was the right
one. Documents are text-layer PDFs only; scans are refused. It's single-user and the live
tryout needs your own key." Then the repo link.

## Before recording
- Pick the exact runs in step 2 to 4 first, and check each one on screen.
- Don't show a key. Don't show your uploaded data.
- If you show `--workspace`, it must be your own sample data only.
