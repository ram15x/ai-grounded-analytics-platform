# Delivery policy

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT written for the Verified Analyst test corpus.

Standard delivery within the United Kingdom is free for orders of £50.00 or more. Orders below that
value pay a flat courier charge of £4.95. Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 working days.

Delivery to EIRE, France and Germany takes 7 to 10 working days and costs £12.50 per parcel up to
10 kg. Each additional kilogram above 10 kg is charged at £1.20.

Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day are dispatched the same day. The postage code POST on
an invoice line is the delivery charge, not a product.
