# Frequently asked questions: returns and delivery

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT written for the AI Grounded Analytics corpus.

Q: Can I change my delivery address after ordering?
A: Yes, until the order is dispatched, which happens the same day if you ordered before 14:00.

Q: How do I get my money back for something I do not want?
A: Send the unused item back; the returns policy gives the time limit and the refund period.

Q: Is there a charge for sending it back?
A: Faulty items cost you nothing. For other items see the returns policy.

Q: Do you deliver abroad?
A: Yes, see the delivery notes for each country.
