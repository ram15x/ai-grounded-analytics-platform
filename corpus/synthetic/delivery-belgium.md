# Delivery to Belgium

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT written for the AI Grounded Analytics corpus.

Parcels to Belgium cost £10.40 each for a weight of up to 5 kg. Each extra kilogram above that is charged at £1.10. Delivery takes 4 to 6 working days from dispatch.

Orders worth £120.00 or more are delivered free of the parcel charge. No customs forms are needed for parcels sent to Belgium.
