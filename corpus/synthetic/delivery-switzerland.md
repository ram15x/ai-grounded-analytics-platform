# Delivery to Switzerland

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT written for the AI Grounded Analytics corpus.

The courier rate for Switzerland is £18.50 per parcel up to 2 kg, plus £3.60 for every additional kilogram. Expect 8 to 12 working days between dispatch and arrival.

There is no free-delivery threshold for this destination. The customer pays any import duty or tax charged on arrival, and the parcel carries a customs declaration.
