# Wholesale and trade terms

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT written for the Verified Analyst test corpus.

Many customers are resellers who buy in bulk. A trade account is opened after a first order of at
least £250.00. Trade customers receive a discount of 15% on any single order above £500.00 and 20%
on any single order above £2,000.00. The discount is applied to merchandise only, never to postage.

Payment terms are 30 days from the invoice date. An invoice unpaid after 45 days puts the account on
hold until the balance is cleared. Minimum order quantity for a trade line is 12 units, except for
items marked as single-unit lines in the catalogue.
