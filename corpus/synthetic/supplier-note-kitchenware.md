# Supplier note: kitchenware

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT written for the AI Grounded Analytics corpus.

Goods in the kitchenware range arrive 28 days after the purchase order is placed. Between October and December the wait grows to 42 days.

The smallest order the supplier accepts is 200 units per line. Invoices are due 45 days after delivery, and quoted prices hold for 12 months. Breakages must be reported within 5 days of receipt to qualify for a credit.
