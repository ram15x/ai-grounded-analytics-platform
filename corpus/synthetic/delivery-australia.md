# Delivery to Australia

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT written for the Verified Analyst test corpus.

The courier rate for Australia is £24.00 per parcel up to 2 kg, plus £5.10 for every additional kilogram. Expect 10 to 15 working days between dispatch and arrival.

There is no free-delivery threshold for this destination. The customer pays any import duty or tax charged on arrival, and the parcel carries a customs declaration.
