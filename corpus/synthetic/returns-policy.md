# Returns policy (current)

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT written for the Verified Analyst test corpus. It describes a fictional company.

Customers may return unused merchandise within 28 days of delivery. The 28 days are counted from the
delivery date shown on the courier record, not from the order date.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 working days of the returned parcel being
scanned in at the warehouse. Opened items that are not faulty carry a restocking fee of 10% of the
item price. Faulty items are refunded in full, including the original delivery charge.

Gift cards and personalised items cannot be returned unless they are faulty. A customer who wants to
send back an item should quote the invoice number printed on the packing slip.
