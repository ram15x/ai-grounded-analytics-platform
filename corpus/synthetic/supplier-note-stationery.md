# Supplier note: stationery

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT written for the Verified Analyst test corpus.

For the stationery range the supplier's standard lead time is 14 days, rising to 30 days in the peak season.

Minimum order quantity: 500 units per line. Payment terms: 30 days from the invoice date. Price validity: 6 months. Any damage has to be notified within 7 days of the goods being received.
