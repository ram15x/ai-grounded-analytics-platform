# Category notes: candles

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT written for the Verified Analyst test corpus.

Pricing: candles are priced at 2.4 times landed cost.

Stock: when a line of candles falls to 60 units, buyers reorder 240 units. The returns rate for the category should stay below 1.5% of units sold.

Candles are stored below 25 degrees and away from direct sunlight.
