# Supplier note: toys

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT written for the AI Grounded Analytics corpus.

Goods in the toys range arrive 21 days after the purchase order is placed. Between October and December the wait grows to 40 days.

The smallest order the supplier accepts is 250 units per line. Invoices are due 45 days after delivery, and quoted prices hold for 6 months. Breakages must be reported within 14 days of receipt to qualify for a credit.
